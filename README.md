# Computer-Graphics-Project
Bots vs Invaders 



Overview 
An army of Invaders is marching slowly towards our house. But they are unarmed. They think they can capture the house, as we are not well trained to use guns. As time passes, the army plans to increase their marching speed but has zero interest in using weapons. On the other hand, we have a different strategy. We can build shooter Bots. We can deploy 1 Bot at a time. But our resources are limited. We can deploy 8 Bots at maximum. Also, the Bots cannot be sent outside a specific boundary. When they touch an Invader, they get destroyed. But their materials can be retrieved to make a new Bot. The game will be over if at least one Invader reaches the house.
(The project is inspired by the game Plants vs. Zombies, 2009)

Features
1. Add Bot to selected tile (during the selection process the target tile’s color will be highlighted)
2. Choose tile and build a Bot (Bots will shoot at a constant speed and direction of firing will be fixed, their standing position cannot be outside a specific boundary)
3. Invader to be generated automatically (they will march straight towards the house, 8 Invaders at max to stay on the game)
4. Each Invader can be killed with 3 shots from the Bot(s)
5. Player gets +2 points for killing an Invader and -1 for a Bot’s destruction
6. The house wall can survive 4 Invader attacks, 5th will cause game over
7. 4 distinct views for playing.
8. Input functions based on different keys and display buttons.
9. Score, remaining lives, resource count and camera position to be shown on the screen.
